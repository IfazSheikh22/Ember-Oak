Our story

Small pleasures, done properly.

Ember & Oak started with a second-hand roaster in a Bradford lock-up and a simple bet: that a coffee house could care as much about its regulars as its roasts.

Bake at eight every morning, and learn regulars’ orders by their second week. The room grew up around the people who use it from students, harbourside dog walkers, one chess club that flatly refuses to leave.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Not for tables — we’re walk-in only and the room turns over quickly. Parties of eight or more can ring ahead on 0117 496 0321 and we’ll do what we can.

Yes. Laptops are welcome all day, batch brew refills are free before 10am, and the wifi pass is on the chalkboard. Just be fair to others during the weekend rush.

Whole, semi and oat as standard; almond and soya for 40p. Oat is our default because it’s what the espresso was dialled in on — ask for anything else and we’ll happily re-dial.

We do — breakfast meetings, crew catering and celebration spreads, delivered across central Bradford. Email catering@emberandoak.coffee with your date and headcount and we’ll put a spread together.

Two named lots per season — currently a washing-station lot in Ethiopia and a small co-op in Colombia — roasted in-house every Tuesday and rested a week before brewing. The current bags are always on the board by the hatch.

The Ember Post. One email a month.

New bakes before they hit the counter, brew guides worth forwarding, and the occasional secret-menu drop. No spam, ever — we’re too busy roasting.
